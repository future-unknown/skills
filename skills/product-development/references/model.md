# Product development model

## Feedback

Kinds: `problem`, `request`, `observation`, `praise`, `question`.

Statuses: `new`, `reviewed`, `linked`, `dismissed`, `resolved`.

Record who observed or reported what, where, and when. Keep `observed_behavior`, `hypotheses`, and `requested_outcome` separate.

## Product initiatives

Statuses: `idea`, `discovery`, `planned`, `active`, `paused`, `shipped`, `cancelled`.

An initiative states the problem, intended outcome, owner, evidence links, and success measures. `shipped` describes delivery, not proof of outcome.

## Experiments

Statuses: `designed`, `running`, `completed`, `stopped`, `invalid`.

Every experiment needs a falsifiable hypothesis, method, success threshold, and owner before running. A result can support, weaken, reject, or leave the hypothesis inconclusive. Preserve raw evidence separately when it is large or sensitive.
