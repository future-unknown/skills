# Customer development model

## Hypotheses

Statuses: `untested`, `testing`, `supported`, `weakened`, `rejected`, `parked`.

A supported hypothesis has evidence but is not universal truth. Preserve the tested segment and workflow.

## Opportunities

Stages: `identified`, `discovery`, `qualified`, `proposal`, `negotiation`, `won`, `lost`, `parked`.

Qualification must be source-backed. Record problem, workflow, buyer, urgency, and next action independently. `won` requires an evidenced agreement, not optimism.

## Discovery

A dated interview, observation, or workflow review. Capture stated problem, current workaround, consequences, explicit signals, explicit non-signals, and hypothesis impact. Keep quotes only with appropriate permission and context.

## Pilots

Statuses: `proposed`, `scoping`, `confirmed`, `active`, `evaluating`, `completed`, `cancelled`.

A confirmed pilot requires named owner, bounded workflow, success criteria, access and approval plan, start/end expectation, and commercial form. A trial account or positive call is not a confirmed pilot.
