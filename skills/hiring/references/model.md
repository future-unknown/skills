# Hiring model

## Roles

Statuses: `draft`, `approved`, `open`, `paused`, `filled`, `cancelled`.

Approval is explicit. A draft headcount idea is not an open role. Compensation ranges always include currency and period.

## Candidates

Stages: `sourced`, `contacted`, `interested`, `screening`, `interviewing`, `reference-check`, `offer`, `hired`, `rejected`, `withdrawn`, `on-hold`.

Advance only with evidence. An introduction or suggested name is `sourced`, not `interested`.

## Interviews

Statuses: `scheduled`, `completed`, `cancelled`, `no-show`.

Record interviewer, date, structured criteria, evidence, and recommendation. Separate observations from interpretation. Avoid unsupported personality, health, family, age, ethnicity, religion, disability, or other protected-trait inferences.

## Decisions

Outcomes: `advance`, `hold`, `reject`, `offer`, `hire`, `withdraw`.

Record approvers and role-specific evidence. A decision record does not replace legally required offer, employment, consent, or rejection communications.
