# Fundraising model

## Rounds

Statuses: `planning`, `preparing`, `open`, `closing`, `closed`, `paused`.

A round records entity, instrument, target, currency, schedule, check strategy, dilution boundaries, purpose, approvals, and unresolved legal implementation. A target is not committed capital.

## Prospects

Stages: `research`, `queued`, `contacted`, `engaged`, `diligence`, `terms`, `committed`, `passed`, `deferred`.

Keep source claims, fit assessment, evidence quality, relationship state, and next action separate. `committed` requires explicit evidence and amount/currency; a verbal positive signal may still be non-binding.

## Materials

Statuses: `draft`, `in-review`, `approved`, `retired`.

Record owners, reviewers, approval requirements, current revision/source path, and circulation boundary. A complete draft is not approved for circulation.

## Routes

Kinds: `direct`, `introduction`, `event`, `inbound`.

Statuses: `identified`, `requested`, `accepted`, `sent`, `meeting-scheduled`, `completed`, `declined`, `closed`.

Coordinate one route per investor organization unless the user explicitly approves alternatives. Colleagues can be preferred or alternate routes without becoming separate opportunities.
